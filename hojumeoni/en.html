🌐 PC Browser Installation & Search Guide

Search for 'Hojumeoni' in your desktop browser store to install in seconds.

📱
Viewing from a smartphone? Desktop Installation
The Hojumeoni extension runs on desktop browsers. When you are on your PC, search Hojumeoni in the Chrome Web Store. Tap [Copy PC Store Link] below to send the link to your messenger or notes for later.
Google Chrome Official
  • 1 Open Chrome Web Store and search for Hojumeoni (or click button above).
  • 2 Select Hojumeoni and click [Add to Chrome].
  • 3 Click the puzzle (🧩) icon in the top-right and click the Pin (📌) icon next to Hojumeoni.
  • 4 Click the Side Panel (◨) icon or toolbar pin to keep your vault open while browsing!
💡 Chrome Tip: Clicking the pinned icon instantly clips the current webpage title and URL into your vault.
🌊 Microsoft Edge 100% Compatible
  • 1 In Edge, click […] Menu ➜ [Extensions] ➜ [Manage extensions].
  • 2 Turn on 'Allow extensions from other stores' on the bottom left, or click 'Get extensions from Chrome Web Store'.
  • 3 Search for Hojumeoni in the store and click [Get / Add to Chrome].
  • 4 In the extensions menu, click the Eye (👁️) icon to show Hojumeoni on the Edge toolbar or sidebar.
💡 Edge Tip: Pin Hojumeoni to Edge's vertical sidebar for seamless split-screen multitasking.
🐳 Naver Whale Sidebar Optimized
  • 1 In Whale, open […] Menu ➜ [Extensions] ➜ [Whale / Chrome Web Store].
  • 2 Search for Hojumeoni and click [Add to Chrome].
  • 3 Click the '+' button at the bottom of Whale's right sidebar and add Hojumeoni to the sidebar.
  • 4 Enjoy a distraction-free sidebar dual view that never overlaps with active web pages!
💡 Whale Tip: Use Whale's auto-hide or pin feature on the sidebar for maximum research productivity.
🦁 Brave Browser Fully Compatible
  • 1 In Brave, navigate to the Chrome Web Store.
  • 2 Search for Hojumeoni and click [Add to Chrome].
  • 3 Click the puzzle (🧩) icon in the URL bar and Pin (📌) Hojumeoni.
  • 4 Toggle Brave's Side Panel icon to keep your archive open while browsing.
💡 Brave Tip: Operates strictly locally without transmitting data to external third-party servers.
🔄 Real-time Mobile & Desktop Cloud Sync:
Connect the same Google Account in the desktop extension settings and mobile Hojumeoni app. Articles clipped on PC appear on your phone instantly, and vice versa!

User Manual

How to Get the Most Out of Hojumeoni

STEP 01

Tap [Share] in Any App

When you encounter an interesting link, article, or photo in Chrome, YouTube, Instagram, or Gallery, tap the system Share icon.

💡 Tip: Long-press Hojumeoni in the share sheet and tap 'Pin to top' to keep it first!
STEP 02

Save to Pocket in a Flash

A compact saving sheet appears. Edit the title or assign tags (#Recipes, #Tech, etc.), and tap [Save]. Done in 1 second!

💡 Tip: Even without specifying a tag, it safely stores in your default inbox.
STEP 03

Find in Seconds with Fast Search

Type keywords or select a tag pill in the top search bar to filter your library in real-time.

💡 Tip: Tap tag chips to group related discoveries on a single screen.
STEP 04

Secure Cloud Backup on Google Drive

Link your Google account under [Settings] ➜ [Cloud Backup] to safeguard your entire library with a single tap.

💡 Tip: Moving to a new phone? Tap [Restore from Cloud] to recover everything in 1 second.
