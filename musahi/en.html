User Manual

Getting Started with Musahi

STEP 01

Select Enlistment Date & Branch

When launching the app, choose the enlistment date and branch (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, or Social Service). Total service duration calculates automatically.

💡 Tip: Accurately reflects standard service reduction regulations.
STEP 02

Watch Your Progress Daily

Watch your progress bar grow every day and count down the remaining days toward discharge!

💡 Tip: D-Day updates automatically each midnight.
STEP 03

Add Home Screen Widgets

Long-press your phone home screen, choose [Widgets] ➜ [Musahi], and place your preferred widget size (2x2, 4x2) onto your desktop.

💡 Tip: Disable battery optimization for Musahi to ensure punctual updates.
STEP 04

Track Leaves & Promotion Dates

Keep track of promotion milestones and register countdowns for upcoming leaves and furloughs.

💡 Tip: Partners and families can install the app to count down together!
