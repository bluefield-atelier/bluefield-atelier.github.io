Privacy Policy

🎖️ Musahi (무사히) BlueField Atelier Effective Date: September 25, 2026

Musahi - Healthy Every Day, Safely Until Discharge ("Musahi", "we", "us") values user privacy and handles personal information securely in compliance with applicable laws and Google Play Developer Policies. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed by the Musahi app (Package: com.bluefield.atelier.musahi, "App") and how it is safeguarded.

1. Core Operating Principles (Local-First)

Musahi is a device-local (Local-First) application that does not require user registration or account login.

  • Military service profile information, schedules, and financial records entered by the user are stored strictly on the user's local device and are never transmitted to or stored on developer-operated servers.
  • When opening external webpages, resolving bookmark titles, utilizing Naver Cafe features, or using Google ML Kit, communication with external service providers or diagnostic telemetry may occur as described below.

2. Information Processed Locally and Purpose of Use

The following information created or entered by the user may be processed on the local device:

2.1 Service Profile Information

  • Name and profile image
  • Enlistment date and scheduled discharge date
  • Branch of service and military rank
  • Enlistment training camp and assigned unit details
  • Other service-related details entered by the user

Purpose: Used solely to compute service completion rate, D-Day milestones, expected promotion dates, discharge projections, and personalized military countdown info.

2.2 Schedules & Calendar Records

  • Event title, date, and time
  • Location and return location
  • Event status and custom notes
  • Extracted event details from images or text

Purpose: Used for schedule management, local notifications, home screen widgets, and user-initiated schedule sharing.

2.3 Financial & Savings Records

  • Salary receipt history
  • Installment savings payments and deposit accounts
  • Other financial records entered directly by the user

Purpose: Used to calculate estimated monthly salary, total savings contributions, and maturity projections.

2.4 Bookmarks and App Settings

  • Bookmark URLs, titles, and notes
  • Favorites, categorization tags, and application preferences

Purpose: Used for managing bookmark links and maintaining personalized app configurations.

3. Storage Location and Data Retention

  • All data entered or created by the user is stored locally in Room Database, SharedPreferences, DataStore, and app-specific private sandbox storage.
  • No user data is stored on remote developer servers.
  • Locally stored data is retained until the user deletes it, clears app data, or uninstalls the App.
  • Upon app uninstall or clearing app data, local sandbox data is permanently removed and cannot be recovered by the developer.

4. External Webpages and Bookmark Features

Musahi may connect to external servers when the user opens external websites or resolves bookmark titles. In this process, the following technical data may be processed by the corresponding website or host:

  • Requested URL and IP address
  • General device and browser header information
  • Cookies and login session tokens
  • Information entered directly by the user on the third-party website

Data processing on external web services is governed strictly by the respective service provider's privacy policy and terms of service.

5. WebView and Cookies

Musahi may provide an in-app WebView for viewing external web services such as community cafes.

  • The WebView may utilize JavaScript, local web storage, session cookies, and third-party cookies to maintain active login sessions.
  • Cookies and authentication tokens remain strictly between the user's device and the external service provider.
  • The developer does not collect, monitor, or store usernames, passwords, or authentication cookies on developer servers.
  • Clearing app data or uninstalling the App clears WebView cookies and temporary cache files.

6. Google ML Kit and OCR Processing

Musahi incorporates Google ML Kit On-Device Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to extract text from images (such as duty rosters or event schedules) for convenient calendar registration.

🔒 On-Device Local Processing:
OCR target images and extracted text are processed exclusively on the user's local device. Neither images nor extracted text are transmitted to developer servers, nor are raw images sent to Google servers.
  • Extracted text is processed locally in temporary memory while the user reviews and edits it, and is saved to the local Room database only if the user saves the event.
  • The Google ML Kit SDK may transmit non-personal telemetry (device model, OS version, package name, API latency and usage metrics) to Google for diagnostic, security, and performance optimization purposes under Google's Privacy Policy.

7. User-Initiated Sharing Features

Musahi provides features allowing users to share schedules or unit visit details with third-party applications.

  • Share Contents (Optional): Schedule title, date/time, location, military unit name/address, user-written notes.
  • Sharing is executed strictly upon explicit user action (tapping the Share button).
  • Users should ensure that sensitive military security details, unannounced operational schedules, internal military base layouts, or confidential rendezvous points are not inadvertently shared.
  • Subsequent processing of shared content is subject to the privacy policy of the selected messaging or social media application.

8. Third-Party Service Providers

The developer does not sell, trade, or transfer military service profiles, schedules, or financial data to third parties. Integrated third-party components include:

  • Google (Google ML Kit): On-device text recognition and SDK diagnostic telemetry (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Naver & Naver Cafe: In-app WebView community browsing
  • User-Selected Websites: Informational guides and bookmark links
  • User-Selected Sharing Apps: External messengers and communication apps
  • Mapping Services: Unit location display and navigation directions

9. Application Permissions

Musahi requests only the minimal permissions required for operation:

  • Notifications (Optional): To deliver D-Day alerts, promotion milestone notices, and schedule reminders. The app functions without this permission.
  • Exact Alarm Scheduling (Optional): To trigger schedule notifications at precise user-selected times.
  • Photo Picker: When selecting a profile image or OCR document, access is limited strictly to user-selected media files without broad storage access.
  • Internet Access: Required for external webpage browsing, bookmark metadata resolution, map views, and ML Kit telemetry.

10. Data Deletion and Destruction

  • Users can immediately delete any schedule, profile, financial log, or bookmark inside the App.
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing app data immediately destroys all local database records and settings.
  • Because data is not stored on remote developer servers, deleted records cannot be viewed, accessed, or restored by the developer.
