O-NEUL Privacy Policy

O-NEUL (AI Voice Journal) BlueField Atelier Effective Date: September 20, 2026

BlueField Atelier ("we", "us", "our") operates O-NEUL (Package: com.bluefield.atelier.oneul, "the App"). Because O-NEUL is designed to handle personal daily journals and voice recordings, we process your information with care and in compliance with Google Play and Google Cloud policies.

1. Core Principle & Information We Collect

O-NEUL operates primarily on an on-device (local-first) storage model:

  • The App does not require user registration or account creation.
  • We do not operate a user database that collects names, national ID numbers, contact lists, or persistent device identifiers.
  • Recorded voice files, journal entries, sentiment tags, memory candidates, and app preferences remain in private app storage (Room database and private files) on your device.

2. AI Processing and External Transmission

When you explicitly trigger AI analysis, summarization, or memory extraction, O-NEUL sends the text content of your journal, user prompt notes, and previous memory candidates to Google's Gemini API.

  • Raw audio files are NOT transmitted directly to AI servers; only transcribed text is sent.
  • Users who do not wish to transmit data to AI services can continue to use all local recording, playback, writing, and search features without requesting AI organization.
🤖 AI Transparency:
Users should avoid submitting sensitive, confidential, or secret information for AI analysis. For more details, refer to Google Privacy Policy and Gemini API Terms.

3. Photos and Videos

  • Photos and videos selected from your gallery remain in their original location; the App stores only a reference URI to display them.
  • Photos taken via the App are stored under Pictures/O-NEUL, and videos under Movies/O-NEUL on your device.
  • Photos and videos are never sent for AI analysis.
  • Deleting a journal entry does not delete original photos or videos from your gallery.

4. Retention and Deletion

  • Local Deletion: You can delete individual journals, use "Manage my records > Delete all records" in settings, or clear app data in Android system settings.
  • Google Drive Sync Deletion: Drive sync data can be deleted through "Delete Drive sync data" in app settings or via Google Account settings.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling the App removes all local database entries and audio files according to Android OS standards.

5. Backup, Encryption & Google Drive Sync

When you create a manual backup, files are encrypted using AES-GCM with a key derived from your user-provided password.

📁 Scope Disclosure: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata
O-NEUL requests access only to its own hidden Application Data Folder in Google Drive. The App cannot view, modify, or access any other files or folders in your Google Drive.

6. App Permissions

  • Microphone: To record voice journals
  • Camera: To capture photos/videos when requested by the user
  • Notifications: To show recording status and reminders
  • Internet: For user-requested AI analysis, Google sign-in, Google Drive sync, and App Check
  • Receive boot completed: To reschedule journal reminders after device restart
  • Foreground service: To continue recording while the app is in the background

7. Advertising & Tracking

The current version of O-NEUL does not include advertising SDKs, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or user-behavior tracking SDKs.

8. Children's Privacy

O-NEUL is not designed for children under 14 years of age.
