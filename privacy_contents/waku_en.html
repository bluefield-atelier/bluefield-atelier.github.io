Privacy Policy

🇯🇵 Waku Japanese (Waku 일본어) BlueField Atelier Last Updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Waku Japanese (Package: com.bluefield.atelier.waku, "we", "us", or "our") handles and protects your information. Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Information Collection and Storage

Waku Japanese does not require user registration or account creation. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) on external servers.

💡 Stored On-Device: All learning progress, mastery records, and personalized settings are stored locally on your device only.

2. Third-Party Services

We use third-party advertising partners to provide free services. These partners may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic and usage information:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC)
    • Data Collected: Advertising ID (GAID), device identifiers, IP address, interaction data, and diagnostic data
    • Purpose: Displaying personalized/non-personalized ads, analytics, and fraud prevention
    • Policy: Google Privacy Policy

3. Device Permissions

The app only requests permissions that are strictly necessary for its features:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): To deliver daily study reminders set by the user.
  • Vibrate (VIBRATE): To provide haptic feedback during card interactions.
  • Network Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): To load advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): To restore scheduled local reminders after a device restart.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored exclusively on your device, uninstalling the app or clearing application data in device settings will permanently delete all records.

5. Children's Privacy

Waku Japanese does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.
